Data Analytics Internship – Task 19 (Top 10 Products)

This repository contains my submission for Task 19 of the Data Analytics Internship.

Dataset - Sample Superstore Dataset

Objective - The objective was to identify the top 10 products based on total sales and practice ranking and sorting.

Tools Used
1. Microsoft Excel
2. Sample Superstore Dataset
3. GitHub

Product Sales Analysis
1. Used the original Sample Superstore dataset containing Product Name and Sales.
2. Created a separate Product Sales sheet containing unique product names.
3. Calculated total sales for each product using SUMIF.
4. Sorted products from highest to lowest total sales.
5. Checked for ties at the 10th position.
6. Created a Top 10 Products sheet with the final results.
7. Created a bar chart to visualize the top 10 products by sales.

Final Results
1. Total products analyzed: 1,850
2. Top product: Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier
3. Top product sales: $61,599.82
4. Top 10 products were identified by total sales.
5. No tie was found at the 10th position.

Files
1. Task 19.xlsx - Excel workbook containing Raw Data, Product Sales, and Top 10 Products sheets
2. README.md - Summary of the analysis and results

Conclusion - The Sample Superstore dataset was analyzed to identify the top 10 products by total sales. The analysis improved understanding of sorting, ranking, tie checking, and presenting results using a chart.
